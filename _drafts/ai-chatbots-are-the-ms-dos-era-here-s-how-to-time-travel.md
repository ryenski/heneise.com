Thesis: If all you're doing with AI is chatting, you're missing out on a ton of its capability. Level up using tools, scheduled routines, skills

Time travel 1: Connect your tools - connect Gmail, Calendar, any third-party apps you use. Advanced: find MCP tools and teach your agent to use APIs.

Time travel 2: Set up Routines & schedules - have your agent read your email, categorize senders, draft auto-replies, notify you of important messages. 

Time travel 3: Teach your AI to do your job - tell Claude or ChatGPT to learn a skill. Give it step by step instructions and ask it to fill in the gaps. Bonus: Claude Skill Creator
