# Do this one simple thing to leapfrog 90% of businesses in your category

This may be one of the easiest but highest impact tools I recommend to clients. It takes only a few minutes to set up, requires no technical know-how, and can increase your productivity by a huge margin. 

The idea is basic: simply connect your AI (Claude or ChatGPT) with your email and calendar. This one simple connection turns ChatGPT from a chatbot into a personal assistant who can keep you up to date on your day, schedule meetings, notify you of important emails, make sure emails don't fall through the cracks, draft responses, and more.

1. Set up ChatGPT or Claude
2. Connect Gmail
3. Connect Calendar
4. Create a morning briefing routine


Practical applications

Create a morning briefing. Summarize yesterday's messages and events, alert you of upcoming meetings, any emails that may need replies, automatically draft follow-up emails. 

Draft emails

Find follow-ups that may have fallen through the cracks. 

Filter unwanted emails

Unsubscribe from emails from senders that you haven't interacted with


## Advanced

Use Composio to connect multiple Google accounts.

Troubleshooting

ChatGPT sometimes has trouble connecting to your Google tools in voice mode. This is half the reason to use these connections, so that limits its utility. A couple of solutions: 

Switch from "live" to "standard" mode. Live mode uses a full-duplex mode that feels more natural for conversation, but it may not have the same access to connected tools. Standard mode uses a simpler half-duplex mode - that means it can only listen or speak, but not at the same time. It feels less natural, but on the backend it translates your speech into text and uses the same connected tools. The result is that it's able to access your Gmail and Calendar.
